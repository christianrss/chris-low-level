## Interpretation highlights

- The arena baseline is substantially faster for the synthetic same-lifetime workload, but the comparison intentionally gives the heap path ownership/destruction work the arena avoids. This demonstrates a lifetime trade-off, not a general allocator ranking.
- The deliberately naïve last-element quicksort shows its expected adversarial behavior on sorted and reversed data. This is the strongest Day 02 algorithm-research result because operation counts and runtime agree with the theoretical warning.
- The state-vector benchmark makes exponential memory/work growth visible. Increasing qubits from 10 to 18 increases amplitudes from 1,024 to 262,144 and sharply reduces gate throughput.
- The OS compositor and debugger protocol numbers are host-reference baselines only; they are not kernel/display/serial performance numbers.
